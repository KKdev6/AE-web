Cell technology

Two chemistries. One standard.

We build with two kinds of lithium cell and pick the one that suits the job. Both are shown here at true scale.

NMC

21700 cells, 4.5 Ah each. More energy for the weight, so a lighter pack goes further.

  • Our 48V, 51V, 60V, 63V and 72V range
  • Up to 151 km per charge
LFP

32140 cells, 15 Ah each. A very stable chemistry that handles heat well and lasts for thousands of cycles.

  • 60V 15Ah and 60V 30Ah scooter packs
  • All home inverter batteries
  • Rated for 2,500+ cycles to 80% capacity

What the BMS protects against

  • Overcharging and deep discharge of any cell
  • Over-current and short circuit
  • Charging when too cold or too hot
  • Cells drifting apart, through balancing on every charge
  • A backup 80 A DC fuse on every scooter pack

How we check every pack

  • Imported cells, checked under Government of India testing
  • BMS settings read back and confirmed on each pack
  • Every finished pack capacity-tested before dispatch
  • Each pack's test result recorded against its pack number
